Chronic Illness Companion

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

1. About CIC

Chronic Illness Companion (CIC) is a personal health-tracking app developed by LadyLuna21. This policy explains how the current Android beta handles information and identifies features planned for future versions. CIC is not a medical diagnostic or treatment service.

2. Information Used by the App

Depending on what users choose to record, CIC may process symptoms, severity and body locations, sleep, energy, fatigue, cognition, mobility, vital signs, medications, digestive and reproductive health information, notes, and other health-tracking entries. CIC also stores app preferences and settings.

CIC uses Google Sign-In. Google and associated authentication services may process account identifiers, basic profile information, and authentication information. Google Sign-In alone does not upload health records to Google Drive.

3. Local Storage

In the current beta, health-tracking records are stored locally on the user's device. Automatic Google Drive backup is not enabled. CIC does not currently operate a developer-controlled cloud database for users' health-tracking records.

Local records may be lost if users uninstall the app, clear app storage, or lose or replace their device. Manual export and import controls are present but are not yet verified as functional in the Android beta. Users should not rely on these controls as their only backup.

4. Planned Google Drive Backup

CIC plans to support backups in each user's own Google Drive account rather than developer-controlled cloud storage. The intended design includes encryption before upload, a user-controlled recovery key, limited backup-file permissions, and safeguards against developer access to backup contents.

These features are planned and are not active in the current beta. Before release, this policy will be updated to describe actual backup behavior, permissions, security, retention, and deletion controls.

5. Services and Permissions

Google Sign-In involves Google's authentication services. Future integrations may include Google Drive, Android Health Connect, and Apple Health, subject to implementation and applicable user permissions. Mentioning a planned integration does not mean it is currently transmitting health records.

Features such as weather or environmental context may use device permissions or external services when enabled. CIC's permissions and network data flows will be reviewed as features develop.

6. Data Sharing and Sale

CIC is not designed to sell health-tracking records or use them for targeted advertising. Health records are not intentionally uploaded to a developer-operated health-data server in the current beta. Authentication information is processed through relevant Google services. Any future optional data transfers will be disclosed in updated privacy information.

7. Security

Users should protect access to their devices and treat any exported health records as sensitive files. Automatic encrypted Google Drive backup is not yet active. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Retention and Deletion

Health records remain in local app storage until removed through available app controls or until app storage is cleared. Uninstalling CIC or clearing app data may permanently erase local records. Users can manage their Google accounts through Google. There are currently no CIC-created automatic Google Drive backups to delete through this feature.

For privacy questions or assistance understanding available deletion controls, contact Lmgentry21@gmail.com.

9. Children

CIC is not specifically designed for children. Its intended audience and applicable age requirements will be reviewed before broader distribution.

10. Policy Updates

This policy may change as CIC adds features or changes its data practices. The effective date will be updated when material changes are published.
